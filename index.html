Book reader

What is this? Your narrated textbooks, offline. Each book is the real pages plus its narration: tap any word and it reads from there, highlighting the sentence as it goes.

Adding a book: copy the book's reader zip (for example Pathophysiology audiobook - reader.zip) onto this device, then tap Import a book and pick it. It's copied into this app's private storage, so you can delete the zip afterwards. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Listening with the screen off: use the controls on the lock screen or in the notification shade.
