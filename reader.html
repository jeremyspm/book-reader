Book reader Library

What is this? The book's own pages, with its narration ready to play.

Click any word on a page and the narration starts from that word. The sentence being read is highlighted and the page follows along.

Keys: Space plays or pauses. ← and → go back or forward one sentence. On a tablet, the lock-screen controls do the same.

Greyed-out text, like figure labels and tables, isn't read aloud. Your place is remembered.
